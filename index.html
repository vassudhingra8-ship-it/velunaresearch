Designing and Launching Veluna in Australia Using Instagram Only

Research Project · Social Media Marketing Strategy · 2026

Executive Summary

Veluna is positioned as a luxury, sustainable everyday sunscreen designed for Australian conditions, including high UV exposure and active outdoor lifestyles. The strategy emphasises that the “best” sunscreen is not only defined by branding, but by consistent and correct usage driven by product experience and integration into daily routines.

This aligns with evidence suggesting that cosmetic elegance plays a critical role in consumer preference. Public health guidance from Cancer Council Australia reinforces correct usage behaviours, while regulatory frameworks from the Therapeutic Goods Administration define strict requirements for product claims.

Sustainability communication must remain evidence-based and avoid vague claims, as outlined by the ACCC. Therefore, Veluna focuses on verifiable actions such as refill systems rather than generic “eco-friendly” messaging.

Key Objectives (6 Weeks):
  • Build trust-led awareness through educational and sensorial content
  • Convert engagement into measurable trial actions

Assumptions and Method

This research assumes Veluna is a new sunscreen brand entering the Australian market with no existing audience base. The product is positioned as a luxury daily facial sunscreen combining premium user experience with sustainability-driven design.

The strategy is informed by five key evidence areas:

  • Public health guidance (Cancer Council Australia)
  • Scientific evidence on sunscreen benefits
  • Consumer preference research
  • Environmental lifecycle studies
  • Social media marketing frameworks

Market and Regulatory Context

Veluna’s communication strategy aligns with Cancer Council Australia’s recommendations, promoting practical and evidence-based sun protection behaviours rather than exaggerated claims.

The Therapeutic Goods Administration defines strict classifications for sunscreen products, requiring precise and compliant messaging. Additionally, the ACCC emphasises that sustainability claims must be substantiated and not misleading.

Positioning and Persona

Luxury as Habit Support

Veluna positions luxury as a mechanism to support daily sunscreen use. By reducing friction such as greasiness and discomfort, the product encourages consistent application.

Sustainability Strategy

Sustainability is framed through a service model rather than marketing language. A refill and return system provides measurable environmental impact while enhancing user experience.

Target Persona

Emily, a 25-year-old Sydney-based user, represents the core audience. She values premium skincare but prioritises practicality and comfort in daily routines.

Instagram Campaign Strategy

Selected Strategy Model:
  • Contribute – educational content
  • Community – interaction and engagement
  • Convert – trial and purchase actions

SMART Objectives

  • Reach 180,000 users and generate 3,000 engagements
  • Achieve 150 trial actions with 3% conversion rate

Content Strategy

The campaign uses Instagram’s visual nature to demonstrate product texture, application, and real-life usage scenarios. Content formats include:

  • Carousels for education
  • Reels for product demonstration
  • Stories for interaction and feedback

Campaign Phases

  • Phase 1: Foundation (compliance + content setup)
  • Phase 2: Launch (education + product introduction)
  • Phase 3: Optimisation (UGC + retargeting)

KPIs and Monitoring

Performance is measured through Instagram Insights and GA4 tracking, focusing on reach, engagement, and conversion metrics.

Ethics and Compliance

The campaign follows strict ethical guidelines:

  • No misleading health claims
  • Compliance with TGA regulations
  • Evidence-based sustainability communication
